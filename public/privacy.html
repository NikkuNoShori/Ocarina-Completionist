Privacy Policy

Ocarina Completionist · Last updated November 8, 2026

Ocarina Completionist is a free fan-made checklist for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. This page explains what information the site keeps and why.

Without an account

If you don't sign in, your checkmarks and edits are stored only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

When you sign in with Google or with an email and password, we receive and store:

  • Your email address and, for Google sign-in, your name and profile picture as provided by Google.
  • Your save files: which checklist items you've collected, any edits you make, and your display settings (theme, light/dark mode, spacing).
  • Who you've shared a save file with, and the email addresses you've invited.

We use this only to sign you in, keep your progress in sync across devices, and let you share save files with people you choose. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use it for anything else.

Google user data

We request only the basic Google sign-in scopes (openid, email and profile). We use them solely to identify your account. We don't access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your data

Only you, plus anyone you share a save file with (and only that save file). People you invite see your email address. Data is stored with our hosting providers, Supabase (database and sign-in) and Vercel (website hosting).

Deleting your data

You can delete any save file from the Account panel at any time. To delete your whole account and everything linked to it, email nickneal17@gmail.com and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
